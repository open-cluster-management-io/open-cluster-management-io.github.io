---
title: "Open Cluster Management (OCM) Becomes a CNCF Incubating Project"
date: 2026-11-10
toc_hide: true
---

Open Cluster Management (OCM) has been accepted as a CNCF Incubating project by the
CNCF Technical Oversight Committee (TOC). This milestone recognizes OCM's technical
maturity, growing adoption, and contributor community.

Thank you to all the maintainers, contributors, and adopters who helped OCM reach
this milestone!

Read the full announcement on the [CNCF Blog](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/11/10/open-cluster-management-ocm-becomes-a-cncf-incubating-project/).
